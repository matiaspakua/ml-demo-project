How It Works

The inference pipeline runs entirely on-device — from upload to prediction in milliseconds.

📤
Upload
Your image is sent to the server as a multipart form.
🔧
Preprocess
Resized to 224×224 and normalized via preprocess_input.
🧠
Model
MobileNetV2 runs inference — 1 000 class scores are computed.
📊
Results
Top-3 predictions are decoded and displayed with confidence.
